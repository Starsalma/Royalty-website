Jhumkas are probably the most loved earring style in India. They work with a saree, a kurta, a lehenga and even a plain white shirt. But there are a lot of styles, and the right pair depends on your outfit, your face and where you are going.

## The main types of jhumkas

### Temple jhumkas

Antique gold finish with temple motifs and often red and green stones. Made for silk sarees, South Indian weddings and festivals. Browse [temple jewellery](/collections/temple-jewellery/).

### Kundan jhumkas

A kundan stud or top with a gold-tone bell, often with pearl drops. Rich and bridal. See [kundan jewellery](/collections/kundan-jewellery/).

### Oxidised jhumkas

Silver-tone with a darkened finish. Great with cotton, khadi and indigo, and the go-to for Navratri. See [oxidised jewellery](/collections/oxidised-jewellery/).

### AD jhumkas

American diamond stones on the stud and bell. A modern take that suits receptions and Indo-western outfits.

### Chandbali jhumkas

A crescent chandbali on top with a small jhumka hanging below. A good middle ground when you want more presence than a plain jhumka.

## Match jhumkas to your face

- **Round face:** longer jhumkas or ones with a drop above the bell. The vertical line helps.
- **Long or oval face:** wider, rounder jhumkas balance a longer face shape.
- **Heart face:** jhumkas that are wider at the bottom than at the top work well.
- **Square face:** round bells and soft curves soften a strong jawline.

These are guidelines, not rules. If you love a pair, wear it.

## Match jhumkas to your outfit

| Outfit | Jhumka style |
| --- | --- |
| Silk saree | Temple or antique gold |
| Bridal lehenga | Kundan or large temple |
| Cotton kurta | Oxidised |
| Pastel lehenga or gown | AD |
| Navratri chaniya choli | Oxidised, colourful stones |

## Balance with the rest of your jewellery

If your jhumkas are heavy, keep the necklace simple or skip it. If you are wearing a big [necklace set](/imitation-jewellery/necklace-sets/), choose the matching earrings from the set or a smaller jhumka in the same tone.

## Wearing heavy jhumkas comfortably

1. Choose a pair with a **sahara** (ear chain) that hooks into your hair and takes the weight.
2. Use **silicone backs** to hold the earring flat against the lobe.
3. Take them off between functions to give your ears a break.
4. Before a long event, ask the seller about the weight. We are happy to tell you on WhatsApp.

## Keep them looking new

Jhumkas have lots of small parts, so dust and sweat collect easily. Wipe them after each wear and store each pair separately. Our [care guide](/blog/how-to-care-for-imitation-jewellery/) covers the details.
