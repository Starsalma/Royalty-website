A good piece of imitation jewellery can look beautiful for years, or lose its shine in a few weeks. The difference is almost always how it is worn and stored. Here are the habits that matter most.

## 1. Jewellery goes on last, comes off first

Put your jewellery on after makeup, hairspray and perfume, and take it off before you change or wash your face. Chemicals in beauty products are one of the fastest ways to dull plating.

## 2. Keep it away from water

Take jewellery off before bathing, swimming, washing dishes or exercising. Water and sweat work their way under the plating over time. If a piece gets wet, pat it dry straight away.

## 3. Wipe after every wear

A quick wipe with a soft, dry cloth removes sweat and oils before they settle. It takes ten seconds and makes a bigger difference than any cleaning method.

## 4. Store each piece separately

Pieces rubbing against each other scratch the finish and loosen stones. Keep each set in its own soft pouch, a zip bag or a box with separate compartments.

## 5. Keep air out

Air and humidity cause tarnish. Airtight zip bags work surprisingly well. You can add a silica gel sachet to your jewellery box to absorb moisture, especially during the monsoon.

## 6. Store necklaces flat or hanging

Folding or tangling chains can bend the links and stress the joins. Lay heavier necklaces flat and hang lighter chains.

## 7. Clean gently, never scrub

For most pieces, a dry cloth is enough. For [kundan](/collections/kundan-jewellery/), use a soft makeup brush to clear dust from around the stones. Avoid silver dip, toothpaste and polishing liquids, which can strip plating.

## 8. Treat oxidised pieces differently

[Oxidised jewellery](/collections/oxidised-jewellery/) gets its look from the darkened finish. Polishing removes that finish, so only wipe it with a dry cloth.

## 9. Check the fittings

Before an event, check hooks, clasps and screw backs. A small tightening with your fingers can save you from losing an earring halfway through a function.

## Quick reference

| Do | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Wear perfume before jewellery | Spraying perfume directly on jewellery |
| Wipe with a soft dry cloth | Water, soap, toothpaste or silver dip |
| Store in separate airtight pouches | Throwing pieces together in one box |
| Take jewellery off before bathing or sleeping | Wearing it in the shower or the gym |

## The bottom line

Moisture, chemicals and friction cause almost all the damage. Keep those away and your pieces will last far longer. If you wear jewellery every day, lighter [daily wear pieces](/collections/daily-wear-jewellery/) are easier to look after than heavy bridal sets.
