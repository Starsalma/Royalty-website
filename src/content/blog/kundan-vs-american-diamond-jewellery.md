Kundan and American diamond are the two styles people ask us about most. Both look beautiful, but they are made for different moments. Here is a straight comparison.

## What each one is

**Kundan** uses flat, uncut-looking stones set close together in a gold-tone frame, often with pearl or bead drops. In imitation jewellery the stones are glass. The look is traditional and rich.

**American diamond (AD)**, also called CZ, uses faceted cubic zirconia stones that sparkle like diamonds. The setting is usually a silver or rhodium tone, sometimes rose gold or gold. The look is bright and glamorous.

## Side by side

| | Kundan | American diamond |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Look | Rich, traditional, warm | Sparkling, modern, sharp |
| Best time of day | Daytime ceremonies | Evening events |
| Outfits | Lehengas, silk sarees, anarkalis | Gowns, pastel lehengas, sarees, western |
| Colours it suits | Red, maroon, green, pink, gold | Pastels, black, navy, silver, white |
| In photos | Looks rich in daylight | Catches flash and stage lights |
| Care | Keep dry, brush gently | Wipe with a dry cloth, store separately |

## When to choose kundan

- Your wedding ceremony, or a close family member's
- Festivals like Diwali and Eid
- Traditional outfits in deep or warm colours
- When you want a look that feels heirloom

## When to choose American diamond

- Receptions, engagements and cocktail evenings
- Pastel outfits, gowns and Indo-western looks
- Office parties and dinners, where you want sparkle without heaviness
- When you want jewellery that looks great under evening lights

## For brides: why not both?

Many brides wear kundan for the wedding ceremony and switch to AD for the reception. That gives you two very different looks, and both styles get their best setting. See our [bridal jewellery guide](/blog/bridal-imitation-jewellery-guide/) for a full function by function plan.
