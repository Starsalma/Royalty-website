## Imitation earrings for every kind of day

Earrings are usually the first piece people notice, and the easiest one to change your whole look with. This collection covers the styles Indian women reach for most often, from heavy bridal jhumkas to light studs you can wear to the office.

### Styles you will find here

- **Jhumkas:** the classic bell shape, in [temple](/collections/temple-jewellery/), [kundan](/collections/kundan-jewellery/) and [oxidised](/collections/oxidised-jewellery/) finishes.
- **Chandbalis:** crescent designs that frame the face, great with lehengas and anarkalis.
- **Studs and tops:** small, light and easy to wear every day, often in [American diamond](/collections/american-diamond-jewellery/).
- **Drops and danglers:** longer lines that suit sarees, gowns and cocktail looks.

### How to choose the right pair

Match the size of the earring to the neckline. A high or heavy neckline looks balanced with studs or small jhumkas, while an open neckline or a saree leaves room for chandbalis and long drops. If you are wearing a heavy necklace, keep the earrings from the same set or pick a simpler pair in the same tone. Our [jhumka style guide](/blog/jhumka-earrings-style-guide/) goes through this in more detail.
