## The finishing touch for festive and bridal looks

A maang tikka frames the face and pulls the whole outfit together, which is why it is part of almost every bridal look. It works for guests and festivals too, especially with a centre parting.

- **Bridal tikkas** have a larger centre piece, sometimes with side chains. See our [bridal collection](/collections/bridal-jewellery/).
- **Light tikkas** with a small pendant suit [festivals](/collections/festive-jewellery/), mehendi and pooja days.
