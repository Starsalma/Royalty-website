## Necklace sets that do the matching for you

A set takes the guesswork out of getting ready. The necklace and earrings are designed together, so the stones, the tone of the metal and the proportions already work as one look.

### Pick by style

- **[Kundan sets](/collections/kundan-jewellery/)** have flat, glass-set stones and a rich, traditional look. A popular choice for brides and close family.
- **[American diamond sets](/collections/american-diamond-jewellery/)** sparkle like diamonds and suit receptions, cocktail parties and gowns.
- **[Temple sets](/collections/temple-jewellery/)** carry motifs from South Indian temple art and pair naturally with silk sarees.
- **[Antique finish sets](/collections/antique-jewellery/)** have a softer, matte gold tone that looks like heirloom jewellery.

### Pick by occasion

For your own wedding, look at the [bridal collection](/collections/bridal-jewellery/). For a friend's sangeet or reception, a lighter kundan or AD set is usually enough. Our [bridal jewellery guide](/blog/bridal-imitation-jewellery-guide/) explains how to plan jewellery for every function.
