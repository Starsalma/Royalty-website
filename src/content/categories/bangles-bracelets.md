## Bangles, kadas and bracelets

Wrist jewellery ties an outfit together, especially in photos. A pair of kadas can carry a festive look on its own, while a full bangle stack is part of many bridal traditions.

### What to look for

- **Size first.** A bangle that is too loose slides off, and one that is too tight is hard to wear through a long function. Check the size on each listing, or ask us.
- **Stack in the same tone.** Mixing antique gold and shiny gold can look uneven. Keep one finish across the stack.
- **Match the occasion.** Heavy [kundan](/collections/kundan-jewellery/) kadas suit weddings, while slim bracelets work for [daily wear](/collections/daily-wear-jewellery/).
