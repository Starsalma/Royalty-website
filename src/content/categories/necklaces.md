## Necklaces you can style your own way

Buying a necklace on its own gives you more freedom than a full set. You can pair it with earrings you already love, layer two pieces together, or keep it as the only statement piece.

### Popular shapes

- **Chokers:** close-fitting and eye-catching, perfect with off-shoulder blouses and boat necks.
- **Long haars:** traditional long necklaces that look beautiful over sarees. Often seen in [temple jewellery](/collections/temple-jewellery/).
- **Pendant necklaces:** a single focal stone on a fine chain, easy to wear to [parties](/collections/partywear-jewellery/) and dinners.
- **Layered necklaces:** two or three strands in one piece for a fuller look without extra effort.
