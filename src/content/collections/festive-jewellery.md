## Dress up for the festival

Festivals are when traditional jewellery really comes out. A pair of [jhumkas](/imitation-jewellery/earrings/), a [maang tikka](/imitation-jewellery/maang-tikka/) and a [temple necklace](/collections/temple-jewellery/) cover most festive looks.

For Navratri, [oxidised jewellery](/collections/oxidised-jewellery/) is the classic choice. For Diwali and family poojas, [kundan](/collections/kundan-jewellery/) and [antique gold](/collections/antique-jewellery/) pieces feel right at home. Need help picking earrings? See our [jhumka style guide](/blog/jhumka-earrings-style-guide/).
