## Jewellery for every function, not just the wedding

A wedding is rarely one event. There is the haldi, the mehendi, the sangeet, the wedding and the reception, and each one needs a different feel. Imitation jewellery makes it practical to have a separate look for each.

- **Wedding day:** a heavy [necklace set](/imitation-jewellery/necklace-sets/), a statement [maang tikka](/imitation-jewellery/maang-tikka/), jhumkas and a bangle stack.
- **Reception:** [American diamond](/collections/american-diamond-jewellery/) pieces sparkle under evening lights and suit gowns and lehengas.
- **Mehendi and haldi:** lighter pieces, often floral or [oxidised](/collections/oxidised-jewellery/), that you will not mind getting a little messy.
