## One or two statement pieces are enough

Party looks work best when one piece does the talking. Choose between a bold pair of earrings or a statement necklace, then keep everything else simple.

- **[Earrings](/imitation-jewellery/earrings/)** in long drops or chandbalis catch the light when you move.
- **[Rings](/imitation-jewellery/rings/)** with a large centre stone add sparkle without competing with your outfit.
- **[American diamond jewellery](/collections/american-diamond-jewellery/)** reads well in evening light and in photos.
