## Designs rooted in South Indian tradition

Temple jewellery takes its motifs from temple carvings: deities, peacocks, lotuses and mango shapes. It was traditionally worn by classical dancers and brides, and it looks best with Kanjeevaram and other silk sarees.

Start with a long [necklace](/imitation-jewellery/necklaces/) or a pair of temple [jhumkas](/imitation-jewellery/earrings/), then add a [maang tikka](/imitation-jewellery/maang-tikka/) for weddings. Read our [jhumka style guide](/blog/jhumka-earrings-style-guide/) for pairing ideas.
