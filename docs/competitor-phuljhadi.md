# Competitor Analysis: phuljhadi.com

Analysed 27 September 2026. Phuljhadi is a **B2C retail** imitation jewellery store on Shopify, so it is the closest model for Royalty (Kanhai Jewels, analysed earlier, is B2B wholesale).

**Method.** Traffic figures are Similarweb estimates, which can be off by 30–50% for small sites. Sales signals come from the store's public Shopify data: the full catalogue (`/products.json`, 2,202 products) and the store's own **best-selling** sort order. That order is driven by real sales, so it is the best public proxy for demand. It shows ranking, not order counts or revenue.

## What people buy

### Sitewide best sellers
1. **Earrings at ₹699** dominate the top 30 (stone drops, jhumkas, German silver, oxidised, pearl studs).
2. **Choker / necklace sets at ₹750** (stone chokers, oxidised sets).
3. **Sunglasses and sunglass covers** rank surprisingly high, as a side category.

Nearly every best seller carries a **bundle tag: "3 earrings for ₹1000"** or **"3 necklaces for ₹1500"**. The multi-buy offer, not the single piece, is what moves volume.

### American diamond collection best sellers
- AD choker and necklace sets: ₹1,280–2,800
- AD jhumkas and earrings: ₹1,160–1,920
- AD studs and rings: ₹480–680, the add-on items
- Almost every item shows a crossed-out "MRP" about 60% above the selling price.

### Catalogue shape

| Signal | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Price spread | Median ₹999. 1,067 of 2,202 products sit at ₹500–1,200 |
| Discounting | 54% of products show a compare-at price. A named sale runs almost every month (Black Friday, Christmas, Mother's Day, Freedom, Holi, Diwali, clearance) |
| Most-used tags | earrings, necklace sets, wedding, handmade, oxidised, kundan, American diamond, haldi/mehendi, lightweight earrings |
| Stock | Only 4 products fully sold out, so they either restock fast or hold deep stock |
| New arrivals | 20–130 new products every month |

## Lessons for Royalty

1. **Price where the volume is:** earrings at about ₹699, sets at about ₹750. Royalty's butterfly and bow pendant sets fit this band.
2. **Bundles beat single products.** Built as `/bundle-offer/`: customers pick any 3 pieces and send the list on WhatsApp.
3. **Earrings first.** They are the volume category, so stock the most earring designs.
4. **Honest discounts only.** Inflated "MRP" anchoring can breach Indian consumer-protection rules and erodes trust. Use genuine bundle and festival offers instead.
5. **Festival calendar.** Rakhi alone drives search traffic for them. Publish festival pages 4–6 weeks ahead (Rakhi, Navratri, Diwali, Karva Chauth, wedding season).
6. **Pinterest.** It is their top social source. Royalty already outputs Open Graph tags for Rich Pins.
7. **NRI demand.** 17% of their traffic is from the USA. Worth offering international shipping if the business can.
